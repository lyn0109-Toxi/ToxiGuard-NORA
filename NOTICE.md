# Notice

ToxiGuard NORA EarlyTox is a research and decision-support prototype.

It does not:

- certify that a product is safe;
- determine that animal testing is unnecessary;
- guarantee FDA, EMA, CTA, IND, NDA, or other regulatory acceptance;
- replace a qualified toxicologist, pathologist, pharmacokineticist, regulatory scientist, or multidisciplinary development team;
- convert missing evidence into negative evidence.

All high-impact outputs must be reviewed within a clearly defined Context of Use by appropriately qualified experts.
