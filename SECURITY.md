# Security Policy

## Prototype boundary

NORA v0.4 is a research and decision-support prototype. The public repository and default deployment are intended for synthetic, public, or de-identified evidence only.

## Do not upload

Do not upload sponsor-confidential, patient-identifiable, trade-secret, export-controlled, privileged, or regulated records to an unapproved public deployment.

## Required controls before confidential pilots

- authenticated users and role-based access
- organization/tenant isolation
- encryption in transit and at rest
- approved secret management
- configurable retention and deletion
- immutable access and decision audit
- data-processing agreements where applicable
- vendor and subprocessors review
- incident response and breach notification
- backup and disaster recovery
- vulnerability and dependency scanning

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability involving confidential data or an exploitable security weakness. Contact the repository owner privately through the contact information in the project profile.
